Our Story

Chicken Doesn't Have to Be Small

The Lollipop Coop was built around one idea: give people something bigger, juicier, and more satisfying than a wing. We take whole drumsticks, prepare them lollipop-style, and build every menu item to hit harder and eat cleaner than anything else at the event.

Blending street-style energy with a premium finish, we show up ready — for breweries, festivals, corporate events, and any crowd that's hungry for real food.
